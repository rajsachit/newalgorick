Refund Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Terms of Service Privacy Policy Risk Disclosure Refund Policy

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for paid Subscriptions purchased from ALGORICK TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED (“ALGORICK”). It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Scope

This policy applies only to fees paid directly to ALGORICK for Subscriptions and add-ons on the Platform. It does not apply to brokerage, exchange charges, taxes or any amount paid to your Broker, and it does not cover trading profits or losses.

2. Free trial

Where a free trial is offered, you can use the included features without charge for the trial period. You can cancel at any time during the trial and will not be charged. If you do not cancel, your selected paid plan will start when the trial ends.

3. Subscription billing

  • Subscriptions are offered on monthly, quarterly or annual plans, as shown on the pricing page.
  • Fees are charged in advance in Indian Rupees, including applicable GST.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless cancelled before the renewal date.
  • We will send a reminder by email before an annual plan renews.

4. Cancellation

  • You can cancel your Subscription at any time from your account settings or by emailing info@algorick.com from your registered email address.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. You will keep access to paid features until the end of your current billing period.
  • Cancelling a Subscription does not close open positions in your Broker account. Please stop your Strategies and review your positions before cancelling.

5. When you are eligible for a refund

You can request a refund in the following cases:

  • First purchase. If you request a refund within 7 days of your first paid Subscription and no live orders have been placed through the Platform during that period.
  • Duplicate payment. If you were charged more than once for the same Subscription.
  • Charged after cancellation. If you cancelled before the renewal date but were still charged.
  • Extended service failure. If the Platform was unavailable for more than 72 consecutive hours due to a fault on our side, in which case we will refund the affected period on a pro-rata basis or extend your Subscription, at your choice.

6. When refunds are not given

  • Partly used billing periods, after the 7-day first-purchase window.
  • Renewals that were not cancelled before the renewal date, except as stated above.
  • Trading losses, missed trades or Strategy performance.
  • Brokerage, taxes or other charges levied by your Broker or the exchanges.
  • Interruptions caused by your Broker, exchanges, your internet connection or devices, or events beyond our control.
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for breach of our Terms of Service.

7. How to request a refund

Email info@algorick.com from your registered email address with:

  • your full name and registered mobile number;
  • the transaction or invoice number and payment date; and
  • the reason for your refund request.

Refund requests must be raised within 7 days of the relevant charge.

8. Refund processing

  • We will review your request and reply within 5 working days.
  • Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days. Timelines may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • Refunds include the GST charged on the refunded amount. Payment gateway charges, where applicable, may be deducted.
  • We will issue a credit note for any refunded GST invoice.

9. Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank so we can resolve the issue quickly. If a chargeback is raised for a valid charge, we may suspend your account until it is resolved.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes apply to purchases made after the updated policy is posted on this page.
